Secondary & Senior Secondary

Quality Public Education for Class 9th through 12th

Established under the Department of Education, Bihar, Uchcha Madhyamic Vidyalaya Hathauri Uttari serves the students of Baheri block. Our school does not run primary or middle grades; our physical infrastructure, laboratories, and specialized teachers are solely dedicated to secondary matriculation and intermediate senior secondary examinations.
